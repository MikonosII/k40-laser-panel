; grblHAL settings for the K40 (SKR Mini E3 V3 + this panel)
; The firmware in release/grblhal already uses these as its defaults. Paste them only if the
; SKR ran grblHAL before (old settings are kept), or after a $RST=* if things look wrong.
; Send one line at a time from LightBurn's Console. Lines starting with ; are notes.

; --- Laser ---
$32=1
$30=1000
$31=0
$33=1000
; ($33 = PWM frequency in Hz. 1000 suits most K40 supplies; try 2000-5000 if low power is uneven.)

; --- Inputs ---
; E-stop and cycle start are unused, so they're inverted to read "inactive" when nothing is wired.
; Feed hold (PWR-DET) is NOT inverted: the lid switch closes to GND when the lid is shut,
; and an open lid (or no switch) reads as feed hold.
$14=68
; Water flow switch on PROBE closes to GND while water flows; no flow = Pn:P.
$6=0
; Not fitted the lid / water switches yet? Use these instead, or the panel shows Lid Open / No flow:
;   $14=70
;   $6=1

; --- Motion (stock K40: 0.9 degree motors, 20-tooth MXL pulleys, 1/16 microstepping) ---
$100=157.48
$101=157.48
$110=18000
$111=18000
$120=2000
$121=2000
; Motor current, mA RMS (TMC2209). Raise in steps of 100 if a motor skips; most K40 motors are fine at 600-800.
$140=600
$141=600

; --- Work area (stock K40 bed) ---
$130=300
$131=200

; --- Homing (optional, only once the end stops are wired to X-STOP / Y-STOP) ---
; K40 heads home to the back left: X toward 0, Y toward max.
;   $22=1
;   $23=1
