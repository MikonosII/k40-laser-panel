; grblHAL settings for the K40 (SKR Mini E3 V3 + this panel)
; The firmware in release/grblhal already uses these as its defaults. Paste them only if the
; SKR ran grblHAL before (old settings are kept), or after a $RST=* if things look wrong.
; Send one line at a time from LightBurn's Console. Lines starting with ; are notes.

; --- Laser ---
$32=1
$30=1000
$31=0
$33=1000
; ($33 = PWM frequency in Hz. 1000 suits most K40 supplies; try 2000-5000 if low power is uneven.)

; --- Inputs ---
; E-stop and cycle start are unused, so they're inverted to read "inactive" when nothing is wired.
; The defaults assume the optional lid and water switches are NOT fitted yet:
$14=70
$6=1
; Fitted them (both close to GND: lid shut, water flowing)? Then send these, so an open lid
; pauses the job (feed hold, Pn:H) and no water flow shows as Pn:P:
;   $14=68
;   $6=0
; Nothing is wired to Z-STOP on a K40, and an unconnected input reads as triggered, so Z is inverted:
$5=4

; --- Motion (stock K40: 0.9 degree motors, 20-tooth MXL pulleys, 1/16 microstepping) ---
$100=157.48
$101=157.48
; Top speed 100 mm/s. G0 moves and LightBurn's travel moves run at this speed; at 300 mm/s
; stock K40 motors skip and lose position. Raise in steps of 1000 if yours stay smooth.
$110=6000
$111=6000
$120=1000
$121=1000
; Motor current, mA RMS (TMC2209).
$140=800
$141=600
; Keep the motors on between moves. With the grblHAL default (25 ms) they let go after every
; move and the gantry can be pushed off position.
$1=255

; --- Work area (measured on a stock K40, 5 mm short of the right and front frame) ---
$130=316
$131=221

; --- Homing (end stops on X-STOP / Y-STOP) ---
; K40 heads home to the back left: X toward 0, Y toward max. X and Y home together, no Z.
$22=1
$23=1
$44=3
$45=0
$46=0
$25=1000
$27=3

; --- Limits and reporting ---
; Soft limits stop any move past the work area (needs homing first).
$20=1
$40=1
; Hard limits off: the K40's long end-stop wires pick up noise and trip ALARM:1 mid-move.
$21=0
; Report work position (what LightBurn expects with the origin below).
$10=0

; --- Origin: 0,0 at the front left (send once, after $H; it's saved) ---
;   $H
;   G10 L2 P1 X-315 Y-221
; Then LightBurn: Device Settings > Origin = front left, work area 315 x 220,
; and Start From = Absolute Coords.
